# Moon Sweepers Wiki

[moon-sweepers.org](https://moon-sweepers.org) is an independent fan wiki for Moon Sweepers, the lunar cleanup game by DragDrop Games.

Start with a toothbrush and a Moon covered in dust. Collect material, deliver it to a station and spend the earnings on better tools, carrying capacity and vehicles. Play alone or with up to 16 players, then connect robots, conveyors and processors to automate parts of the cleanup.

## Your first cleanup route

Finish a collection-and-delivery loop near a station before buying equipment. A better broom collects more; a larger container reduces return trips. A Packager unloads hand-tool dust onto a belt, while a robot vacuum cleans around a dock.

Once a dust route works, consider processing. A Pressure Chamber turns dust into the same mass of sand. A Stone Press needs both sand and fresh dust, so keep an unprocessed branch. Higher sale prices do not guarantee sustained output: supply, power and clear belts matter.

Start with the [beginner guide](https://moon-sweepers.org/guides/beginners-guide), [Packager setup](https://moon-sweepers.org/guides/how-to-use-packager) and [automation guide](https://moon-sweepers.org/guides/automation-guide).

## Common questions

### Can I play solo?

Yes. Single-player and online co-op with up to 16 players are supported. A crew shares the host's world, wallet and matrix; purchased vehicles belong to their buyer.

### Why is my Stone Press stopped?

Check raw dust, sand, sufficient power and a clear exit. Turning every capsule into sand removes a required ingredient. See the [Stone Press guide](https://moon-sweepers.org/guides/automation-guide#how-the-stone-press-works).

### Does the map read my save?

No. Its visited checklist stays in your browser and does not change your world or synchronise with the crew.

### How do I protect a save?

Exit the game and copy the current world and backup folder before attempting recovery. Check the [Windows backup location and slot rules](https://moon-sweepers.org/guides/save-recovery-guide).

### Are calculator results guaranteed earnings?

No. They describe selected inputs and assumptions. Gross sales and processing value added differ; downtime, supply and transport can reduce actual output.

### Is this the official game site?

No. This fan wiki is not affiliated with or endorsed by DragDrop Games, the publisher or Valve. Use the [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5335510/Moon_Sweepers/) for purchases and developer announcements.

### Income and upgrade planning

[Selling and Income](https://moon-sweepers.org/tools/selling-and-income) explains material prices, payout bonuses, processing value and exploration rewards. The [Income Calculator](https://moon-sweepers.org/tools/income-calculator) keeps launch sales, full-container estimates and region rewards separate.

Use the [Upgrade Matrix](https://moon-sweepers.org/tools/upgrade-matrix) to browse all 23 paid nodes and their prerequisites. The [Upgrade Calculator](https://moon-sweepers.org/tools/upgrade-calculator) adds individual levels, shared prerequisite costs and eligible Supplier Network discounts in purchase order. An unresolved alternative prerequisite is shown as a choice, rather than silently included at an invented price.
