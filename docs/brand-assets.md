# Brand assets

- Social preview: public/images/og-image.png, 1200 × 630 PNG.
- Original vector logo: public/images/logo.svg.
- Browser icons: public/favicon.svg and public/favicon.ico (16, 32, 48, 64, 128 and 256 px).
- Apple icon: public/apple-touch-icon.png, 180 × 180.

The vector mark was authored in SVG: an ice-blue crescent, small craters, an orange sweeping trail and a broom on a navy rounded field. It is independent site branding rather than the official game logo.

The social illustration was generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then resized to the required output dimensions with Sharp. The original generated image remains in the Codex generated-images directory. No CLI image-generation fallback or API key was used.

## Generation prompt

Create a polished wide 1200 by 630 social preview card for an INDEPENDENT fan wiki about the lunar cleaning game Moon Sweepers. Original stylized 3D illustration, deep midnight navy space, crisp pale blue lunar craters and orange-gold industrial equipment, one friendly small astronaut sweeping a curved clean path across moon dust on the right half, small conveyors in far background, cinematic warm rim lighting. Left half typography with exact clear text 'MOON SWEEPERS' large white geometric sans serif on two lines, small orange label 'WIKI & GUIDES', smaller pale blue text 'Machines · Maps · Factory Setups'. Tiny footer 'moon-sweepers.org'. Generous 65px margins, all text safely away from edges. A tasteful original small crescent-moon-and-sweep-stroke emblem beside the wiki label. Sophisticated legible game editorial design, strong hierarchy, navy and orange palette. No Saturn rings, no official game logo, no claims of being official, no watermark, no UI screenshots. Illustration is concept art, not gameplay.

## Media boundary

This artwork does not expand the permission for official screenshots or community equipment images. Those assets retain their local-development-only handling. Generated concept illustration must not be labelled as gameplay.
